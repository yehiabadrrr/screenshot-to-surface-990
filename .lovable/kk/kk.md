# Markazly — launch film style bible

## Source material
- **Brand (wins every conflict):** Markazly identity canvas. Navy leads, teal points. Navy #142B4A, Brand blue #2864A6, Teal #19A7A0 (center point and live/active moments only), Teal 700 #0E7C77, Navy 950 #0B1A2E, Charcoal #182230, Slate #667085, Off-white #F5F7FA, White, Amber 700 #A15C07 (pending), Rust 700 #B4441A (overdue). Manrope 700/800 display (−2% tracking), Inter UI with tabular figures, IBM Plex Sans Arabic. Symbol: an M whose center point (teal dot) sits in a concentric orbit cut at the vertex. Voice: calm, precise, numeric ("Know what you collect. Know what you're owed.").
- **Ref A, fintech dashboard:** lilac → periwinkle studio gradient, a white app window tilted ~20° in 3/4, one saturated hero object (glossy blue card) overlapping the UI. Expensive because: real UI density + perspective + one saturated hero object, everything else quiet.
- **Ref B, healthcare floating spheres:** violet world full of spheres in 4 materials (glossy violet, matte white, pearl, frosted), heavy depth of field, a frosted glass pill with a toggle and a pointer, a tilted paper document. Expensive because: material variety, real DOF, and a glass chip that refracts what's behind it.
- **Ref C, tech explainer:** deep electric blue gradient (#0A2366 → #2E6FE6), a device with UI, sculptural props (ribbed spheres, bumpy iridescent cluster, cyan torus arc) at the frame edges. Expensive because: sculptural 3D props with rim light framing a calm center.

## Palette by scene (brand-mapped; the refs' violet becomes navy/ice-blue)
- Dark scenes: Navy 950 #0B1A2E → Navy #142B4A → Brand blue #2864A6 glow; teal is the single hero accent.
- Light scenes: Off-white #F5F7FA → ice #DDE7F3 → periwinkle #B9CCE8 haze; navy objects, one teal moment per frame.

## Lighting
Analytic studio: key softbox upper-left-front (large, warm-neutral), strip fill camera-right (long, dim), rim softbox high behind (cool, bright) giving a clean edge line on every object. Clear-coat on ceramics, filmic tone map, no blown whites (key ≤ 0.9 after tone map).

## Materials
Matte titanium (navy M, phone frame), glossy teal ceramic (center point), frosted glass (chips, panels), iridescent pearl + matte white + glossy navy spheres (Ref B), ribbed navy lantern spheres + teal torus arc (Ref C), lacquered navy→blue gradient slab (Ref A's card, re-cut as a "Collected" card).

## Camera
3/4 tilts of 15–25°, objects float with no floor; soft contact shadows only where a surface exists; slow push or orbit always on; foreground props pushed into DOF blur.

## Type
Manrope 800 display at −3% tracking, Inter 500/600 for UI and figures (tabular), Plex Arabic for one bilingual line. One headline per scene, ≤ 5 words, the brand's point-as-full-stop in teal.

## Composition
Hero object on a third, UI fragments as illustration (no stock devices beyond one phone), huge low-contrast background type for depth, glass pills and chips floating in front.

## The recurring mark
Markazly's own symbol (not a new one): extruded matte-titanium navy M, glossy teal ceramic sphere as the center point. It opens the film, rides as a tiny embossed mark on the card and phone back, and closes the film.

## Story — "Your center. Under control." (~30 s, 6 scenes)
1. **The center point (0–5 s, dark, Ref C):** a teal sphere bounces in through ribbed spheres and a teal torus arc; a touch taps it and it snaps into the vertex of the titanium M. "Your center." Transition: the torus flies through camera, iris reveal.
2. **Know your numbers (5–10.5 s, light, Ref A):** tilted Overview dashboard, a lacquered "Collected" slab counts to EGP 182,400; touch on "Record payment" → toast drops in. Transition: whip pan.
3. **Every installment, tracked (10.5–15.5 s, ice-blue, Ref B):** floating receipt + glass status chip in a sphere field; touch flips Due → Paid, amber to teal. Transition: tap ripple floods to navy.
4. **Before the session, not after (15.5–20.5 s, navy):** titanium phone in 3/4; reminder notification drops in with a haptic wiggle; touch "Send to 12 parents" → sent. Transition: push into screen.
5. **Teacher shares, calculated (20.5–25.5 s, light):** teacher payout cards fanned in depth, attendance ring fills to 91%, a card swipes to "Paid out". Transition: bloom to white.
6. **Under control (25.5–30.5 s, white → navy finale):** the M + center point lock up with the wordmark; "Your center. Under control." + Arabic line; touch presses "Book a demo".

All names, people and figures are illustrative and come from the brand canvas's own sample data.
