# Markezly launch film: three storylines

Status: waiting for a pick. Nothing is built yet.

Facts used (from the app and the landing page, nothing invented): "Mark attendance. The billing does itself." /
"سجّل الحضور… والحساب بيتعمل لوحده."; fees monthly, per session or by package; cash, InstaPay, Fawry, bank transfer
with a reference; pending transfers confirmed when the money arrives; corrections are reversals, never edits;
teacher pay by salary, session or percentage of collections; every screen in Arabic, right to left. All product
shots are captured from the running app with its sample data (Nile Academy, labelled as sample).

Assumptions for all three: 30–40 s; 1080×1920 (9:16) master for Reels, recomposed (not squeezed) to 4:5 and 16:9;
an Arabic version and an English version from the same composition (text and captures swapped by variables);
no voiceover; music either the original score from the first Markezly launch film (free, ours) or a track added
in Instagram, with a HeyGen catalog track as the paid option.

## A. One transfer, followed (recommended)

**Device / form:** one object across apps / object-to-object chain.

A parent's EGP 850 InstaPay transfer arrives as a chat message and, instead of sinking into a pile of screenshots,
we follow that exact payment through Markezly: recorded with its reference, waiting as pending, confirmed when the
money lands, counted in today's collections, and finally in the teacher's percentage. The viewer feels the relief of
one number that never gets lost.

- **First 2 s:** frame 0 is a chat bubble on navy, "حوّلت ٨٥٠ على إنستاباي" with a transfer receipt thumbnail, and the
  line "Where does this transfer go?" already on screen.
- **Beats:** hook (the transfer arrives) → tension (it drops into a pile of month-end screenshots) → reveal (the
  receipt becomes the Record payment form, "Markezly" lands) → proof (method and reference, Save → ledger row Pending,
  Confirm → Completed) → payoff (850 flies into "Collected today", then into the teacher's 40% line) → claim
  ("Every pound, traced.") → end card (the M mark is carried in by the 850 chip, "Book a demo on WhatsApp").
- **Look:** brand navy ground, the app's white surfaces punched in at 1.6–2×, teal rationed to the 850 amount and
  the end card; Manrope and Inter, IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic.
- **Sound:** near-silence with phone buzzes in the pile, a calm 100 bpm pulse opens on the reveal, clicks on real
  clicks, one soft chime on "Confirmed", the music lifts once for the payoff.

## B. 6:00 PM, the register

**Device / form:** a moment in someone's day / one continuous take.

The camera never cuts: from a phone at a classroom door at 6:00 PM, where the front desk marks Physics · Prep 3
(present, present, absent…), it pulls out through the phone into the desktop console, glides to Finalize ("17
charges"), down to the student balances, and out to the owner's dashboard at 9:00 PM. The viewer feels a whole
evening of admin handled without anyone doing maths.

- **First 2 s:** "6:00 PM" in huge type and a phone already showing the real attendance roster, a thumb tapping
  Present.
- **Beats:** hook (6 PM, the register) → tension (the paper notebook that nobody bills from, ticked and forgotten)
  → reveal ("Mark attendance. The billing does itself.") → proof (Absent marked, Mark all, Finalize → 17 charges
  posted, balances update) → payoff (9 PM, the owner's dashboard complete) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** the ground moves through the evening, afternoon blue to night navy, as time stamps advance; the app
  stays the constant white surface; type is the time stamp.
- **Sound:** a ticking 110 bpm pulse that follows the clock, taps on every mark, a low swell on Finalize, the
  music resolves at 9 PM.

## C. فين الفلوس؟ (the bold one)

**Device / form:** type carries the argument / match cuts on the beat.

Arabic-first and loud: giant Egyptian-Arabic words from the landing page's three pains ("كشكول الغياب", "سكرين شوت",
"النسبة") each match-cut into the real right-to-left Arabic screen that answers it: the notebook word becomes the
register, the screenshot word becomes the payment form with its reference, the percentage word becomes the
teacher's pay line. The viewer feels the product speaking their language, literally.

- **First 2 s:** "الفلوس بتضيع فين؟" filling the frame in heavy Arabic type, the last word's dot already teal.
- **Beats:** hook (the question) → three pains, each a word that becomes its screen (tension and proof
  interleaved) → reveal ("سجّل الحضور… والحساب بيتعمل لوحده.") → payoff (all three screens collapse into the owner's
  dashboard) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** full-bleed colour fields (navy, white, teal) changing on the half beat, heavy IBM Plex Sans Arabic,
  the app's Arabic RTL screens; English subtitles small in the English cut.
- **Sound:** punchy 120 bpm percussion, a hit on every match cut, a drop on the reveal.
